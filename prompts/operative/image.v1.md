This is a still image.

There is no camera move and no action over time — describe a single frame.

Work these into the description, only where the prompt leaves them open. They
are qualities of the sentence you write, not fields to list.

Framing and shot size, and where the subject sits in the frame. The light by
its source, direction and hardness. Lens or depth of field only when it changes
what the viewer attends to. Surface and texture where they carry the image.

Never describe movement, duration, or anything that happens next.
